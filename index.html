About Us

Church history and mission since 1985

Founded in 1985, A.C.K St Peter's Gacharage Church has been a beacon of faith in Ruaka, Kiambu County. From humble beginnings with 20 members, we've grown to serve over 500 families through worship, fellowship, and community service.

Our journey reflects God's faithfulness, marked by milestones like the construction of our first sanctuary in 1992 and the launch of youth ministries in 2005.
