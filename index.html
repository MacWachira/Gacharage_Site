About Our Church

Gacharage Anglican Church has been serving the community since 1985, dedicated to spiritual growth, fellowship, and service.

Our History & Mission

Gacharage Anglican Church was established in 1985 with a vision to spread the Gospel and serve the community. Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant congregation of over 500 members, dedicated to spiritual growth and community service.

Our mission is to make disciples of Jesus Christ through worship, fellowship, and service. We strive to be a beacon of hope and love in our community, reaching out to those in need and sharing God's grace.

We believe in the power of prayer, the authority of Scripture, and the importance of community. Our church is part of the Anglican Church of Kenya, Mt. Kenya South Diocese, and we uphold the traditions and doctrines of the Anglican faith.

Our Leadership

Our church is led by Reverend John Maina, who has served as our pastor for the past 10 years. He is supported by a team of dedicated elders and ministry leaders who work together to shepherd the congregation and oversee church operations.

Church Belts

Our church is organized into geographical belts to better serve our community. Each belt has its own leadership and meets regularly for fellowship and outreach.

RUaka Belt

Community outreach and fellowship in the RUaka area. Our RUaka belt meets every Tuesday evening for Bible study and prayer. They are actively involved in community clean-up projects and supporting local schools.

Meeting: Tuesdays, 5:00 PM at RUaka Community Hall

Leader: Elder James Mwangi - 0722 123 456

Miberethi Belt

Serving the Miberethi community with love and compassion. The Miberethi belt focuses on visiting the sick and elderly, and organizing youth mentorship programs. They also run a small agricultural project to support needy families.

Meeting: Wednesdays, 4:00 PM at Miberethi Primary School

Leader: Deaconess Mary Wanjiku - 0733 456 789

Rumenye Belt

Building faith in the Rumenye region through discipleship and evangelism. The Rumenye belt is known for its vibrant worship and active door-to-door evangelism. They recently started a children's feeding program that serves over 50 children weekly.

Meeting: Thursdays, 6:00 PM at Rumenye Church Building

Leader: Pastor David Kimani - 0712 345 678

Church Development Projects

We are continuously working to improve our facilities and expand our ministry impact. Here are some of the ongoing and upcoming development projects at our church.

New Church Building

Construction of our new worship center to accommodate our growing congregation. The new building will seat 800 people and include modern facilities for children's ministry, offices, and fellowship spaces.

75% Complete - KSh 12.5M raised of KSh 16.5M needed

Community Hall

Multi-purpose hall for church activities, community events, and skills training programs. The hall will host weddings, conferences, and serve as a disaster relief center when needed.

40% Complete - KSh 4.2M raised of KSh 10.5M needed

Children's Playground

Safe and modern playground for our children with age-appropriate equipment. This project will provide a dedicated space for our Sunday School children to play and learn in a secure environment.

25% Complete - KSh 150,000 raised of KSh 600,000 needed
